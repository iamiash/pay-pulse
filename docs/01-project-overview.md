# 01. Project Overview

PayPulse is a full-stack, enterprise-grade web application engineered to solve the operational complexities of tracking recurring digital subscriptions, managing payment accounts, and auditing monthly financial commitments.

### System Scope & Core Pillars
1. **Centralized Subscription Tracking**: Log subscription details including service name, plan tier, billing frequency (Monthly/Yearly), total active months, purchase date, next billing date, auto-renewal state, associated email, and contact phone number.
2. **Encrypted Financial Wallet**: Store bank accounts (Routing Number, Branch, Account Number), payment cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Nexus with Credit/Debit designation and encrypted CVC), and mobile banking accounts (bKash, Nagad, Rocket).
3. **Dynamic Payment Mapping**: Directly link each subscription record to saved bank, card, or mobile banking methods. Includes inline creation modals inside forms.
4. **Automated Notification Engine**: Runs background evaluations on upcoming renewals, generating alerts for services renewing within 7 days.
5. **Reporting & Analytics Suite**: ReportLab-powered PDF generation providing Full Summaries, Subscription Histories, Monthly Audits, and Advanced Date-Range Filtered Exports.
6. **Polished User Experience**: Collapsible sidebar with hover tooltips, profile menu with sign-out confirmation modals, locked DOB account settings, avatar upload/deletion, and universal live search suggestions.
