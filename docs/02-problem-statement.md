# 02. Problem Statement

Modern consumers and professionals rely on dozens of recurring SaaS platforms, cloud utilities, and media streaming services. Managing these subscriptions across multiple payment methods creates severe financial friction.

### Core Industry Challenges
1. **Trial Traps & Surprise Auto-Renewals**: Users frequently sign up for trials and forget renewal dates, resulting in unwanted recurring charges.
2. **Fragmented Payment Channels**: Expenses are scattered across multiple credit cards, bank accounts, and local mobile banking accounts (bKash, Nagad, Rocket), making unified tracking impossible without centralized software.
3. **Security Risks in Note-Taking**: Storing payment references, card numbers, CVCs, or bank account routing numbers in unencrypted plain-text files or paper notebooks exposes users to financial privacy breaches.
4. **Lack of Detailed Reporting**: Standard banking apps provide lump-sum charge logs but lack service-level analytics, tenure counters, or custom date-filtered PDF exports needed for personal or business tax audits.
