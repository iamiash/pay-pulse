# 06. User Survey Metrics

A quantitative survey of 35 active SaaS users provided key data shaping PayPulse's functional priorities.

### Key Metrics
* **Multi-Payment Dispersion**: 88% of respondents use at least 3 distinct payment channels (Cards, Banks, Mobile Banking) for recurring subscriptions.
* **Auto-Renewal Overhead**: 74% reported paying for at least one unused subscription over the past year due to missed manual renewal dates.
* **Export Demand**: 65% rated downloadable PDF reports as an essential capability for personal accounting.
* **Mobile Banking Integration**: 91% of regional users required native support for bKash, Nagad, and Rocket mobile banking tracking.
