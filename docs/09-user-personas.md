# 09. User Personas

### Persona: Lead Software Engineer & Tech Consumer
* **Profile**: Manages 10+ professional and personal SaaS platforms (AWS, JetBrains, Github, Netflix, Canva, Spotify).
* **Payment Setup**: Split between 2 Credit Cards (Visa, Amex), 1 Bank Account (City Bank), and 1 Mobile Banking Account (bKash).
* **Goals**: Centralize expense monitoring, encrypt card CVCs and bank details, receive early billing alerts, and export PDF reports for tax tracking.
* **Pain Points**: Forgetting auto-renewal dates, managing fragmented receipts across multiple cards, and struggling to trace which card pays for which service.
